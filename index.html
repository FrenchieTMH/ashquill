ASHQUILL

An Elder Scrolls companion for your Discord server

Add Ashquill to Discord

Ashquill brings random lore and NPC quotes from the Unofficial Elder Scrolls Pages (UESP) to your server, lets your community test itself with trivia, and keeps a leaderboard so everyone can settle who really knows Tamriel best.

TriviaQuestions with a source shown after every round.
Guess the quoteWho said it? Then try to name the game it came from.
LoreA random lore page, or look one up by topic.
Your questionsSubmit your own trivia and quotes for admin review.

Commands

Commands start with ! by default. Server admins can change it with !setprefix. Type !help in your server for a quick reminder.

Play

In every game, a hint appears if nobody has answered after 15 seconds.

  • !triviaAnswer an Elder Scrolls trivia question. The source is shown after every round.
  • !guessquoteGuess who said a quote, then try to name the game it came from.
  • !guessgameWhich game is this line from? Also works as !whichgame.
  • !trivia skyrimStick to one game in !trivia or !guessquote: skyrim, oblivion, morrowind, eso (or sr, ob, mw).
  • !trivia 5Add a number to any game above to play a match of 3 to 10 rounds in a row. Mix both: !trivia mw 5.
  • !stopEnd the match after the current round (the player who started it, or an admin).

Scores

  • !leaderboardThis server’s top 10. Add trivia, quote or game for one game, or month for this month’s season.
  • !lorerankYour place, points and personal bests (longest streak, fastest answer). !lorerank @name for someone else.

Explore

  • !loreA random lore page from UESP, or look one up by topic: !lore dwemer.
  • !quoteA random NPC quote, or one from a single game: !quote morrowind, !quote eso.
  • !pollStart a poll: !poll Question? | Option 1 | Option 2. With no options, it’s a yes/no poll.

Contribute

  • !submittriviaSubmit your own trivia question. Ashquill asks for the question, then the answer. An admin reviews it.
  • !submitquoteSubmit a quote for Guess the Quote. Ashquill asks for the quote, then the speaker.
  • !reportThink the last answer was wrong? !report <link> <reason>, with a link that proves it.

General

  • !helpShow the list of commands in Discord.
  • !welcomePost Ashquill’s welcome message again.

For server admins

These need the Administrator permission in your server. Type !adminhelp to see them in Discord.

  • !setprefixChange the command prefix for your server: !setprefix ?.
  • !pendingtriviaSee trivia submissions waiting for review, numbered. !pendingquotes does the same for quotes.
  • !approvetriviaApprove or reject a submission by its number: !approvetrivia 2, !refusetrivia 2, !approvequote 1, !refusequote 1.
  • !pendingreportsSee reports of wrong answers. !approvereport passes one on to be fixed; !refusereport rejects it and tells the reporter why.
  • !memberwelcomeGreet new members: on, off, channel, message or test. Off by default.
  • !dailylorePost a lore page every day from noon: on, off, channel or test. Off by default.
  • !rankaliasLet !rank work as !lorerank in your server: on or off. Off by default, so it never clashes with level bots like MEE6 that also use !rank.
